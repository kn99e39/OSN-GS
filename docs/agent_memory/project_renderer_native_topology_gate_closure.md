Worklog 109 (branch `arch/2dgs-coverage-first-surface`) closed [[project_camera_induced_representative_backbone_audit]]'s (WL108) two remaining caveats without modifying WL107's adjacency algorithm.

**CAVEAT 1 (same-forward accounting)**: extended the diagnostic-only CUDA build (canonical `diff_surfel_rasterization` still untouched) with a new per-primitive `out_forward_accepted` flag, captured in the exact same forward execution/loop iteration as WL107's `median_surfel_id` (representative) capture. Real-scene cross-tab: `representative_and_not_forward_accepted = 0` (exhaustive, whole scene) -- representative implies forward_accepted with zero exceptions, structurally and empirically. This resolved WL108's 36,051-surfel discrepancy directly (not by floating-point speculation): all 36,051 (100%) ARE forward_accepted in this same execution, confirming the gap was WL105's separate backward-gradient diagnostic (different CUDA build, divides to reconstruct T) missing a genuine contribution -- not an inconsistency in WL107's own signal.

**CAVEAT 2 (patio/hedge overlap provenance)**: replayed WL107 unmodified (exact reproduction: 36.77% largest component, 45.02% singleton, matches WL107/WL108). Extracted the real graph frontier (1,110 edges, no sampling) connecting the 89,502 hedge-region members (26.2% of hedge total, matches WL108's absolute count) to the rest of the largest component -- median 5 supporting views, 80.7% multi-view supported, comfortably inside the geometric gate's pass range. Ran exact bridge/split-impact analysis (Tarjan bridge-finding + DFS subtree-size, O(V+E)) over the whole largest component (437,751 nodes, 1,034,050 edges, 56,816 bridges total, top-10 each separating >=1% of the component). **Key finding: only 67 of 56,816 bridges (0.118%) cross the hedge/non-hedge boundary, and the worst of those separates only 56 surfels (0.013% of the component)** -- the patio/hedge overlap is NOT a fragile single bridge; it's robustly connected via 1,043 redundant (non-bridge) frontier edges. The component's real structural fragility (bridges separating up to 10.7% of the component) lives entirely inside one region or the other (hedge-internal or non-hedge-internal chains), never at the hedge/patio boundary itself. High-impact bridges are majority multi-view supported (29/40 sampled), not single-view noise.

**GATE: PASS.** Renderer-Native Surface Representative Graph (WL107, audited WL108, caveats closed WL109) is now CANONICAL Visible Surface Topology Backbone. Explicit caveat retained: this purely geometric/renderer-grounded construction captures observed-continuous rendered surface, not semantic object identity -- the largest component genuinely, robustly includes both patio-adjacent structure and hedge vegetation (multi-view corroborated, not an artifact/bug). Semantic (patio vs hedge) separation is out of scope for this topology stage. Non-representative renderer contributors remain unattached support evidence -- still not given any role. **Framing correction (worklog 110, [[project_nonrepresentative_evidence_attribution]]): this was originally worded as "left to a future Trust/semantic stage" -- that is wrong. Trust measures reliability of evidence for latent surface fitting, not object/semantic identity; patio-vs-hedge separation belongs to a future surface decomposition/NURBS-representation stage, not Trust.**
